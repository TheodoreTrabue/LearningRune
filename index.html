How to test handwriting
  1. Open the alphabet below and verify our reference shapes first.
  2. Choose Collect handwriting samples and select a letter.
  3. Draw it naturally, then press Check to see the independent prediction.
  4. Press Save labeled sample if your drawing correctly represents the selected letter. Clear and repeat with 3–5 natural variations.
  5. Include both E and O forms. Try some samples with a mouse and others with a pen, if available.
  6. Use Evaluate saved samples to find troublesome letters. Export samples when you want to share them for improvements.

Samples and overrides do not automatically train the recognizer. Testing with fresh drawings helps reveal mistakes. No stroke order is required.

Write one letter per box, from left to right. Use full E and O symbols: short lines must attach to another letter. Words with EE or OO will come later.

About this experiment

This recognizer compares your drawing against the whole alphabet and a fixed collection of handwriting examples. Your prompt does not influence its prediction.

Natural size, position, and mild slant changes are allowed. Orientation matters; ink color and stroke order do not.

Word practice uses separate boxes. Free-canvas words, nesting, stacking, and drawing English letters come later.
